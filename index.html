Sound Familiar?

Real problems small businesses run into, and what I do about them.

An employee quits on bad terms.

Their email, files, and logins are shut off in minutes, their company phone or laptop can be wiped remotely, and your data stays with you.

A laptop gets left at a gas station.

It's encrypted, so the files can't be read. I can lock or wipe it from here, and you're back up on a new one fast.

Your insurance company sends a cybersecurity questionnaire.

I go through it with you, close the gaps it asks about (two-step login, tested backups, threat monitoring, regular scans), and get you ready to sign.

You need five new computers.

I'll tell you exactly what to buy for your budget, then set every one up the same way so they're ready the day they arrive.

The Wi-Fi drops or the internet crawls.

I find the real cause instead of guessing, fix coverage in the back office, and give customers their own guest Wi-Fi.

Nobody knows how anything is set up.

I map it out and document it in plain English: what you have, how it's set up, and who to call for what. Passwords go in a password manager, never on paper.
